Built for the moment a client asks something hard

Ready-to-read answers

When the client asks, you get the exact words to say, grounded in your own documents. If it is not in your notes, the coach says so instead of inventing it.

One company knowledge hub

Admins upload decks and documents for every business. If a client asks about a product you did not prepare for, the coach finds it there.

Objections handled live

Price, competitor, timing, approval: your prepared answer first, then three ways to respond. Plus silent nudges on filler words, pace and talking too long.
