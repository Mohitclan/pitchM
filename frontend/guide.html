Trying Pitchlight — a 2-minute guide

Pitchlight is an AI speaking coach. It listens while you talk, gives short private nudges, and writes a full review afterwards. This pilot runs on this laptop only — nothing is shared outside it except with the AI services that do the analysis.

Before you start

  1. Pick your name in the top-right “Who's testing?” menu (or add yourself). Your sessions and feedback stay separate from other testers.
  2. Use Google Chrome and wear headphones, so the coach hears only you, not the speakers.
  3. When Chrome asks for the microphone (and camera), click Allow.

Option A — Practice a talk

  1. Open Practice app.
  2. Choose what you're preparing for (pitch, interview, presentation…), then Start Session.
  3. Talk for 1–3 minutes. The coach may interrupt briefly when it spots fillers, fast pace or lost eye contact.
  4. Press End Session and wait about a minute for your review.

Option B — Real or mock meeting

  1. Open Meeting Mode. Pick the meeting type and platform.
  2. Optional: open Pre-call brief and paste the agenda / client notes and the points you must cover.
  3. Join your call in Chrome, then click Choose meeting audio → pick the meeting tab → tick “Share tab audio”, so the coach hears the other person too.
  4. Click Start meeting coach. Drag the floating bubble next to your camera.
  5. When the call ends, click ■ End, then Open transcript & full review.

What you'll see during the meeting

  • Silent nudges in the bubble — “Slow down”, “Answer the question they asked”, “Add energy”. Nobody in the call sees or hears them.
  • 💡 Answer outline — when the other person asks a question, three quick points to structure your answer.
  • Points to cover — ticked off as you mention them, with a reminder near the planned end.
  • Help buttons — 💡 Help me answer (outline for their last question), ↺ Catch me up (what was just said, if you lost focus), ? What to ask (a smart question to move the meeting forward).
  • Client read — a chip shows how the client comes across (engaged, confused, skeptical…), with a tip when it changes.
  • Answer timer — shows how long you've been answering and nudges you to wrap up after 2 minutes; a tip if you talk over the client.
  • You / Them meters — proof the coach can hear both sides. Tips wait while the client is talking.
  • 👍 / 👎 on each tip — tells us which tips are useful.
  • The notch — a small dark pill (status dot, latest tip, progress ring, timer). Place its window at the top centre of your screen once. Tips and answer outlines drop down below it and fold back up by themselves. Tap the pill to peek, ⌄ for details (mood, pace, talk share, meters).
  • ⚙ settings (opacity, size, dim when idle), ▁ shrinks to just the pill, ❚❚ pauses listening, ■ ends the meeting.

What you get afterwards

  • Meeting recap — summary, decisions, action items and a ready-to-send follow-up email.
  • Question-by-question review — each question you were asked, how well you answered, and a stronger sample answer.
  • Score, full transcript, coaching moments and an AI report. Download everything as a .txt.
Please leave feedback: at the bottom of every review there's a short “How useful was this?” form (30 seconds). It decides whether we roll this out to the team.

Good to know

  • If you share your screen in the call, share only the meeting window or a tab — not your whole screen — so the bubble stays private.
  • Text appears a second or two after you pause; speaking with natural pauses works best.
  • Sessions under ~25 spoken words aren't scored.
