Your coach, inside any meeting

A small floating coach bubble sits on top of Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or any call. It listens to you and shows silent nudges only you can see. When the meeting ends, the full transcript and review are saved to your History.

Pre-call brief (optional) — tell the coach what this meeting is about

The coach ticks off points as you cover them and reminds you of anything missed near the end.

Rehearse this call 2 min The AI plays the other side and asks the hardest questions from your brief. Answer out loud.
Hear the other person too (recommended)
So the coach knows the questions you're answering. Pick your meeting's browser tab and tick “Share tab audio”, or pick your whole screen with “Share system audio” on Windows.
Not connected — only your voice will be coached.

  • Wear headphones so the coach only hears you, not the other people.
  • Drag the bubble anywhere on your screen — it stays on top of your call.
  • Keep this tab open until the meeting ends. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  • Nudges are text only — nobody in the call can hear or see them.
