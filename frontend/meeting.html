Test mode: try a practice meeting first Tested ✓

A built-in dummy client, Priya, asks 11 realistic questions out loud while you present a sample script. It runs exactly like a real meeting, so you see every feature work: script follow-along, answers from documents, objection cards, nudges, commitments and the final review. About 12 minutes. Nothing is added to your real clients, documents or progress.

Your coach, inside any meeting

A small floating coach bubble sits on top of Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or any call. It listens to you and shows silent nudges only you can see. When the meeting ends, the full transcript and review are saved to your History.
