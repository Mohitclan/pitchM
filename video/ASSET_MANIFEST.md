# Asset manifest

Every product image in the films is a screenshot of the **real PitchMirror pages**. They were served by the local Docker stack at `localhost:8080` and captured by `capture/capture.mjs` at 1920×1080, device scale 2.

**What was simulated during capture**
- **Demo data.** The session data is a fictional story (a startup called "Brightpath" pitching and selling to "Northwind Dental"), defined in `capture/demo-data.mjs`.
- **Mocked inputs.** The data is played into the pages through a mocked WebSocket and a mocked `/api/sessions`. **No Gemini or Claude call was made**, and nothing was written to the database.
- **Browser stand-ins.** These replace things a headless browser doesn't have:
  - Chrome's fake camera, fed a plain dark frame (`assets/media/camera-off.y4m`).
  - A synthetic tone for "meeting tab audio", which plays only while the client speaks.
  - Face tracking turned off.
  - The notch in its on-page form, because automation cannot screenshot the always-on-top window; it uses the same DOM and `bubble.css`.
  - The page note set to the text Chrome shows when the notch floats in its own window.
- **Real settings.** "Dim when idle" was switched off in the notch's own settings.
- **Fresh profile.** A new browser profile with tester "Me" was used, so no real sessions or tester names appear.

**What is real**
- All layout, labels, buttons, states and transitions come from the product's real code: `app.js`, `meeting.js`, `bubble.css`.
- The scorecard, recap, Q&A review and email are rendered by the real review code from the demo data.
