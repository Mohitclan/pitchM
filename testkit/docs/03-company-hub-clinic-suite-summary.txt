Clinic Suite is practice management software for dental clinics. It handles appointment booking, patient charting, treatment plans and automatic appointment reminders by text and email. It is sold per clinic per month. Clinic Suite costs 199 US dollars per clinic per month and includes onboarding. It works with Billing Hub through a one-click sync, so completed treatments appear in Billing Hub as ready-to-bill items without re-typing. Clinic Suite is a separate product with its own team, but customers can buy both together at a ten percent bundle discount.
