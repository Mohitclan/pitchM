Pitchlight extension

The extension listens to the meeting tab you start it on and sends that audio to your Pitchlight meeting page, so the coach hears the other person too. Your own voice still comes from the microphone in Pitchlight.

Open Pitchlight in

How to use it

  1. Join your meeting in Chrome (Google Meet, Teams or Zoom on the web).
  2. On the meeting tab, click the Pitchlight icon and press Start coaching on this tab, or press Alt+Shift+P.
  3. Pitchlight opens beside the meeting. Press Start meeting coach there.
  4. When you end the meeting in Pitchlight, the extension stops listening.

Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. The Zoom and Teams desktop apps aren't covered: join from the browser instead.
